Do this before you type anything. You are not guessing whether they need it, you are looking.
1Search the business name on Google Maps
Is there a listing at all? If there is, look for “Claim this business” or “Own this business?” — that means Google generated it and nobody owns it. That is scenario B and it is the usual one.
2If nothing is there, search «their industry» in «their area»
Look at the box of three businesses Google puts above the website results. Whoever is in it is getting the calls, and your client is not one of them. Only needed for scenario A.
3Screenshot whichever one shows the gap
The box with them missing from it, or their half-empty listing. Send the screenshot with the message. The gap does the selling, you do not have to argue.
